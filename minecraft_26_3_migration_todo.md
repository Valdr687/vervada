## Modded/Wiki Additions Catch-up
Based on the recent changelogs (Chaos Cubed, The Copper Age, Spring to Life, Chase the Skies, Tiny Takeover, Bundles of Bravery, etc.), several new blocks and mechanics require custom models or stylistic adjustments to fit the medieval theme:

- [ ] **The Copper Age**:
  - [ ] **Copper Bars**: Needs models (exposed, weathered, oxidized) consistent with iron bars.
  - [ ] **Copper Chains**: Needs models (exposed, weathered, oxidized) consistent with standard chains.
  - [ ] **Copper Chest & Large Copper Chest**: Verify if block/entity models need adjusting for the medieval style.
  - [ ] **Copper Lantern**: Needs models (exposed, weathered, oxidized) similar to standard hanging/standing lanterns.
  - [ ] **Copper Torch**: Needs models similar to standard/soul torches.
  - [ ] **Shelves**: New storage blocks crafted from stripped logs (oak, spruce, birch, dark oak, crimson, warped, mangrove, bamboo, cherry, pale oak). Needs models fitting the medieval aesthetic (like bookshelves).
- [ ] **Chaos Cubed**:
  - [ ] **Cinnabar Block Set**: Add models for `cinnabar`, `polished_cinnabar`, `cinnabar_bricks`, and `chiseled_cinnabar`, along with their stair, slab, and wall variants.
  - [ ] **Sulfur Block Set**: Add models for `sulfur`, `polished_sulfur`, `sulfur_bricks`, `chiseled_sulfur`, and `potent_sulfur`, along with stair, slab, and wall variants.
  - [ ] **Sulfur Spike**: Stalactite/stalagmite. Needs models for its 4 stages (base, middle, frustum, tip).
- [ ] **Bundles of Bravery**:
  - [ ] **Redstone Components**: Redstone torch, comparator, and repeater models and textures were updated in Vanilla Java Edition. Verify if the pack overrides them and update them to match the new standards if necessary.
